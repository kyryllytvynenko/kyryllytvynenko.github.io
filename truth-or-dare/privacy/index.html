Privacy Policy

Truth or Dare · Last updated 17 October 2026

Truth or Dare is a pass-the-phone party game made by Kyryl Lytvynenko, an individual developer. This policy covers the web version at kyryllytvynenko.github.io/truth-or-dare and the Android app on Google Play.

The short version

  • The game doesn’t collect anything about you. No accounts, no sign-up, no analytics.
  • Player names, game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data from your device to serve them. If you buy “Remove ads”, Google Play handles the payment.

What stays on your device

The names you type in, whose turn it is, which cards have been drawn and your settings are saved in your browser’s storage (web) or the app’s storage (Android), so a game can pick up where it left off. This data never leaves your device and isn’t sent to me or anyone else. You can erase it by clearing the site’s data in your browser, clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Web version

The web version shows no ads, sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties — the fonts and every card are bundled with the game. It’s hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when your browser requests the pages, for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Android app: ads

The Android app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google’s ads software in the app may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • information about ad interactions, app performance and crashes.

This data is collected and used by Google, not by me — I can’t see it or tie it to you. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you’re in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google’s consent message before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the app’s settings. Wherever you are, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings.

Android app: “Remove ads”

You can pay once to remove ads. The purchase is processed by Google Play: I never receive your name, card details or other payment information — only confirmation from Google Play that the purchase exists, so the app knows to stop showing ads. Google’s privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

Children

Truth or Dare is made for adults and isn’t directed at children. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because I don’t collect or store personal data, there’s nothing I hold about you to access, correct or delete. For data Google collects to serve ads, use the device settings above or Google’s My Ad Center.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
